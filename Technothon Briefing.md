# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

- What is the main focus of the competition?
    
    UM Technothon focuses on case study problem solving combined with prototype building, allowing participants to develop practical and innovative solutions.
    
- What skills can participants gain from this competition?
    
    Participants will gain experience in problem-solving, teamwork, innovation, technical development, and real-world project execution.
    
- Do participants need to be from engineering or computer science backgrounds?
    
    No. Students from any discipline are welcome, as the competition encourages multidisciplinary collaboration and diverse perspectives.
    
- When will the competition take place?
    
    25th April - 6th June 2026. The competition runs across several phases, ending with the physical final day on 6th June 2026.
    
- Is UM Technothon 2026 free to join?
    
    Yes. A RM 25 commitment fee is required, but it will be fully refunded after successful submission for all rounds.
    
- Who can participate?
    
    UM Technothon 2026 is open to all undergraduate university students in Malaysia, including diploma students.
    
- How many members can a team have?
    
    Each team can consists of 1-5 members.
    
- Is the competition online or physical?
    
    The competition will be conducted in both formats. The preliminary round and mentoring sessions will be held online, while the final pitching day will be conducted physically at University Malaya.
    
- Is the competition focused on hardware or software?
    
    Teams may focus on hardware, software, or a combination of both. While there is no limitation, participants are encouraged to adopt a balanced integration of hardware and software where relevant.
    
- Are digital prototypes acceptable?
    
    Yes. Both digital and physical prototypes are accepted.
    
- What do teams need to submit for the preliminary round?
    
    Teams **must** submit a pitch deck and a video presentation. **Optional** submissions include a visual prototype (such as Figma) and a GitHub repository.
    
- Will participants receive mentorship?
    
    Yes. Participants will have access to **online** mentoring sessions during the prototype development phase
    
- Do teams need to build a working prototype?
    
    Yes. Finalist teams will need to develop their prototype during the building phase **before** presenting in the physical final day.
